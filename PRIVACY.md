# Privacy Policy: SBC Solver for Web App

Last updated: October 21, 2026

SBC Solver for Web App is a browser extension that works inside the EA SPORTS FC Ultimate Team Web App. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

## What the extension reads

On the Web App pages (ea.com) only, while you use them, the extension reads:

- the players in your club, SBC storage, unassigned items and transfer list (ratings, clubs, leagues, nations, positions, EA's price shown for each card);
- the SBCs you open (requirements, formation, current squad);
- your active squad, so its players can be left out when you choose that option.

## What it stores

In your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on your device only:

- your settings (the options in the extension's panel and Auto Complete dialogs);
- small caches that make it faster: formations and brick layouts it has seen, and the estimated values of SBC tiles.

You can delete all of it by removing the extension.

## What it sends

Nothing leaves your browser to the developer or to any third party. There are no analytics, trackers, ads or accounts, and the solver runs entirely inside your browser.

The extension only talks to EA, through the Web App's own functions, and only when you use one of its features: loading an SBC's challenges, searching EA's concept players (if you turn that option on), saving a squad you asked it to fill, and submitting SBCs when you press **Complete**. Those requests go to EA exactly as if you had done them in the Web App yourself, under EA's own privacy policy.
